How this comparison was produced and its limits

Source: session-3516-335313015-0, capture-1, 2560 × 1440. The previous WorldGI matches QA build 0.3.216-test.20261102.1. Color, depth, normals, original lighting, AO, fog and materials come from the capture.

The GI-probe atlas was not exported in the QA capture. It was reconstructed by the production solver from the local world cache: a 20 × 20 × 14 grid, 64 rays and 3 bounces, without dynamic actors or previous probe history. Both GI shaders receive the same reconstructed atlas. Their difference passes through the production blur and is added to the captured lighting. WorldComposite and MaterialComposite then run.

This reconstructs the change to a single frame. Motion, temporal history accumulation and the missing stencil mask are not reproduced; dithering is neutral. Small effects and edges may therefore differ from the game. The previous replay differs from the QA capture by a mean of ERROR_MEAN levels out of 255; the 99th percentile is ERROR_P99 and the maximum is ERROR_MAX. Two reference regions on distant walls match exactly in RGB.

The fix uses directional shadowing as a bounded approximation of ambient lighting where GI-probe coverage is insufficient. It can also deepen outdoor shadows beyond probe coverage. With full coverage, the GI result is preserved.
