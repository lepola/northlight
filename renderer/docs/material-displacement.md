# Signed material height and displacement

This extension uses the inferred material height for both GPU shading and bounded CPU subdivision. Height .5 is the authored surface: lower values recede and higher values protrude. Automatic interpretation centers luminance and raises sparse warm/green accents. Bright-high/dark-high overrides and surface balance are available. Color remains a heuristic; it cannot establish the physical shape of a painted material.

AutoBumpTerrain, AutoBumpWalls, AutoBumpGrass and AutoBumpModels accept Off (0), Normal maps (1), Normal maps plus POM (2), or Displacement plus normal maps (3). Mode 3 does not also raymarch POM. Models share one selector covering weapons, body/clothing atlases, characters, creatures, mounts and props; the renderer cannot reliably classify these as separate item categories. Additive spell/particle programs and unsupported inputs retain native rendering.

AutoDisplacementStrength 0..1000 controls a .12-yard total span at 100; distance is 4..24 yards and detail 1..5 sets a texture-density subdivision target, bounded to 128 subdivisions per edge and the existing mesh limits. Models have separate strength 0..100 (default 25, a .03-yard span), distance 2..16 and detail 1..4. Grass is capped at sixteen subdivisions per edge. AutoHeightPolarity is auto/bright-high/dark-high (0/1/2), AutoHeightDetails is 0..100 and AutoHeightBalance is 0..100. Defaults and preset values are in the quality INI. Restart after standalone INI edits; the combined menu integration supplies live controls.

Signature-gated SM2/SM3 programs supply original UVs and bone deformation. Subdivision precedes the original skinning shader. Bone IDs merge by identity, active normalized byte weights sum to 255, and rigid models ignore unused skin attributes. Native combiners preserve original alpha. Native and material replay use matching displaced geometry; UP/indexed bindings and shader constants are restored.

Ordered CPU uploads, aligned rectangle edits and successful full GPU copies update a bounded 4 MiB/128-entry atlas cache. It retains 128x128 averages from atlases up to 1024 pixels without GPU readback. Unknown partial writes invalidate CPU height. GPU-only render targets can receive normal/POM shading but require a valid CPU snapshot for geometry. The shared upload-cache infrastructure also supports the separate particle-light work.

Geometry has an adaptive 32..128 MiB/256-mesh budget and at most two builds per frame; resident snapshots, bounds and height budgets are 32/8/2 MiB. World-loading pressure reduces geometry to 32 MiB and snapshots to 8 MiB, with separate recovery thresholds. Generated normal maps use 32..96 MiB/256 entries, at most two generations per frame and a separate 128 MiB bound on retained source textures. Proven four-bone templates use cached pose envelopes; other templates have an 8,192-source-vertex bounds budget per frame. Over-budget draws keep native geometry with normal maps. Seams and boundaries are pinned, large draws reduce subdivision within 32,768 triangles, and geometry fades near the range limit. Raster depth and silhouette change; collision and static shadow/GI cache meshes remain authored.

Prepare client fixtures with scripts/bump_recon.py, tests/validate_auto_bump.py and tests/render_auto_material_oblique.py --compatible-compiler (omit the flag with the pinned compiler). Then run tests/validate_displacement.py, tests/validate_model_materials.py and tests/validate_displacement_overlay.py with --backend pointing to a plain D3D9/DXVK DLL. tests/validate_material_height.py extracts its exact Silvermoon texture directly; tests/validate_native_material_alpha.py checks mounted M2 combiners. tests/validate_material_stream.py checks replay order and partial atlas updates. tests/test_material_geometry.py covers CPU subdivision, resident snapshots and atlas bounds. Client shaders, textures, captures, previews and DLLs stay in ignored output. These synthetic checks do not measure in-game FPS or establish visual coverage in every scene.

Local follow-up: model relief uses local high-frequency albedo detail to avoid inflating broad painted body regions. Geometry reuse keys only constants used by UV programs; unchanged full/partial atlas uploads keep their revision. Normal maps and retained source references have separate bounds. Under world-loading pressure normal maps use a 32 MiB budget and displaced meshes a 32 MiB budget, recovering with hysteresis; POM and displacement stay enabled. MSAA uses a private single-sample capture depth, restores the original depth/targets and retries transient allocations.

Skinned displacement also tapers across the first triangle ring at UV, material and unsupported-skin boundaries. A triangle whose original vertices all lie on a boundary stays on the authored surface. Previously its subdivided interior could still move at full height, inflating thin eye shells and facial seam triangles around the eyelids and chin while native overlays stayed in place. The same interpolated boundary weight scales CPU offsets and GPU-height directions. Interior relief, inferred normal/cavity maps, native colour and animation remain active; rigid/world displacement is unchanged. See [m2-face-displacement-20261031.md](m2-face-displacement-20261031.md) for offline checks and their limits.

Material shadow rays stop at exact saturation. Consecutive normal-map lookups use a bounds-checked recent entry while preserving texture domain and revision checks. Displacement preparation reuses UV-key storage and compares cheap cache fields before geometry bytes; built meshes own their immutable key. These changes preserve configured quality.

Repeated byte-identical UP draws reuse a source snapshot before rebuilding bounds, after matching the retained declaration, vertex program, domain, topology and all source bytes/indices. Current pose constants, UVs and height revisions still participate in preparation. Both displaced raster draws and their binding restoration remain separate.

Private displacement index buffers use 16-bit storage when the generated vertex range fits, retaining the 32-bit fallback and charging the actual uploaded byte count. Index values, primitive order and subdivision are unchanged. CPU boundary round trips and the cache fixture cover both the format decision and allocation sizes.

Skinned displacement preparation retains a certified float constant bank and refreshes a conservative span covering changed 16-register blocks. Invalid or changed sources, failed/raced reads, Reset and static captures drop that certificate. Fixed-function or unknown draws and ProcessVertices invalidate both the epoch and mirrored constant values; reentrant setters, getters and draws cannot establish a false proof. Unavailable providers retain full reads and their error handling. Pose, UV and height evaluation remain unchanged.

The existing bounded declaration cache supplies immutable elements and validated layouts to both preparation and its private snapshot reader. Retained COM references prevent address reuse from selecting an old layout. Only a new displaced output declaration needs a mutable copy; Reset clears the private cache. The state-reuse regression and actual mirrored model fixtures cover partial writes, stale proofs, lifecycle changes and prepare/draw/restore sequences.

Displacement cache lookups shortlist source and exact material candidates, then retain all original declaration, program, material, revision, UV and full-source comparisons. Source fingerprints are sampled hints; standard float hashing preserves UV equality, and full comparisons reject collisions. Insertion order preserves UP canonicalization and overlay ties; optional-index allocation failure uses the ordered cache. Geometry budgets charge each uploaded mesh and each distinct retained immutable source once, with the same limits, LRU policy and pressure recovery.

The periodic `DISPLACEMENT snapshots` diagnostic reports cumulative attempt totals, UP/buffered counts and rejection reasons, including unknown failures. It does not change admission or memoize failures. Compare rates in matching scenes; older logs lack this reason distribution.
