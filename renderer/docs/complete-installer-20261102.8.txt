Changes since .7
- Preserve pressure caps while avoiding another cache clear when the previous
  completed trim did not grow the largest free region and memory is stable.
  Aggregate shortage, builder demand and a smaller largest region still permit
  a trim. A new episode remains eligible when the prior cooldown expires.
- Attribute sync-storm transitions to locks, unlocks, queries, other calls and
  express texture readbacks. Buffer/texture readbacks are overlapping explanatory
  subsets, not additional round trips. Existing counters are sampled at the
  two-second detector boundary.
